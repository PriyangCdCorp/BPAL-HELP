# Product – Tabs and Sections

The Product Notebook includes multiple tabs for organizing different types of information:

**Available Tabs**
- **Properties:** Contains basic product information such as code, description, category, and type
- **Scaling:** Manages size, weight, or dimension scaling parameters
- **Test:** Contains testing criteria and quality assurance information
- **Other:** Miscellaneous additional product information and properties
- **Delivery Point:** Information related to delivery locations and logistics
- **Product Group:** Classification and grouping information for the product
- **Unit Of Measure:** Definition of measurement units used for the product
- **Audit:** Historical tracking and audit trail information about product changes

Each tab organizes related fields logically, allowing users to navigate between different categories of product information.
