# Faq

BPAL (Bulk Procurement and Logistic Management System) is an integrated software and hardware system developed by CDCorp. It is designed to automate and manage the raw material supply chain for industries such as pulp, paper, bioenergy, and recycling.

BPAL connects administrative software with physical hardware (like truck scales, gate readers, and dumpers) to create a contactless, automated workflow for receiving bulk materials. Its main goals are to reduce operational costs, eliminate human error, ensure worker safety, and provide real-time business intelligence.
