# Knowledge Base – 23. Maintenance and Next Training Modules

This knowledge base should be versioned as the BPAL walkthrough continues. For every new function, record: (1) where the user encounters it, (2) permissions and prerequisites, (3) exact action sequence, (4) changes to the database versus container, (5) validation/errors and edge cases, (6) interactions with saved configurations, (7) a user-facing Q&A, and (8) confirmed vs pending points. Test answers against actual BPAL screenshots and user scenarios before deploying an assistant.

Future modules should cover the rest of notebook-level icons and Info details; specific Product tab validation; Vendor/Client primary-location administration; Contract business workflows; Transaction, pay-period, scale and quality-analysis workflows; security administration; and deployment- or site-dependent features. Never conflate confirmed standard behaviour with incomplete specialized workflows.
