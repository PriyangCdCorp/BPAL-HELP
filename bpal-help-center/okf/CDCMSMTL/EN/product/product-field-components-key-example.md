# Product – Field Components - Key Example

In the "Properties" tab shown:

**Code:** An open field for entering the product code (e.g., "012")

**Description:** Contains bilingual descriptions (French and English) with an open field format where users can enter or edit product names

**Status:** A closed field showing whether the product is "Active" or "Inactive" - this is typically system-managed

**Category:** Displays the product category (e.g., "Recycle Payable") - may be open or closed depending on system configuration

**Type:** An open field with a lookup icon, allowing selection from a list of product types (e.g., "BROWNGRADE")
